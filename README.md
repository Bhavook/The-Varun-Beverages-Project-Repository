## Executive Summary
This repository contains a comprehensive 10-year multi-stage Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation model and equity research analysis for Varun Beverages Limited (NSE: VBL), one of the largest franchisees of PepsiCo outside the US. The model forecasts Unlevered Free Cash Flow (UFCF), incorporates capital structure dynamics to calculate WACC, and determines the intrinsic value of the stock.

**Investment Verdict: Overvalued / Sell**
*   **Current Market Price (as of Sept 3, 2026):** ₹407.00
*   **Calculated Intrinsic Value:** ₹161.66
*   **Margin of Safety:** Negative
*   **Implied Downside:** ~60%

Based on the fundamentals and projected cash flows, the current market price implies aggressive growth expectations that exceed historical anchors and sustainable long-term metrics. 

## Key Valuation Assumptions
The model utilizes a three-stage operating block approach to accurately capture growth deceleration as the company reaches maturity.

*   **Cost of Capital (WACC):** 14.30%
    *   *Risk-Free Rate:* 6.95%
    *   *Equity Risk Premium (ERP):* 7.08%
    *   *Beta:* 1.05
    *   *Cost of Equity (Ke):* 14.38%
*   **Terminal Growth Rate:** 5.00%
*   **Revenue Growth Trajectory:** Tapering from 18.5% in FY26E to a terminal state of 10.0% by FY35E.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Peaking at 25.0% in FY30E before stabilizing at 22.0% in the terminal years.
*   **Capital Expenditure:** Modeled as a percentage of sales, tapering from 14.0% (FY26E) down to maintenance levels of 5.0% (FY35E).
